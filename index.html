About the Series

MONEY HEIST

A Global Phenomenon

La Casa de Papel (Money Heist) is a Spanish television heist crime drama series created by Álex Pina. The series traces two long-prepared heists led by the Professor (Álvaro Morte), one on the Royal Mint of Spain, and one on the Bank of Spain.

Worldwide Impact

The series became one of the most-watched non-English language series on Netflix, winning the International Emmy Award for Best Drama Series in 2018. It has influenced pop culture worldwide with its iconic red jumpsuits and Salvador Dalí masks.

The Philosophy

At its core, Money Heist is about resistance — a group of outcasts fighting against the system. The series explores themes of love, loyalty, betrayal, and the blurred line between good and evil.
