About the Series

What is Money Heist?

La Casa de Papel (known internationally as Money Heist) is a Spanish heist crime drama series created by Álex Pina. It originally premiered on Spanish network Antena 3 on May 2, 2017, and was later acquired by Netflix, which distributed it globally. The series became Netflix's most-watched non-English language series and one of the most-watched series overall on the platform.

Behind the Scenes

Production Details

Concept & Creation

Álex Pina conceived Money Heist as a limited series for Spanish broadcaster Antena 3. The concept of using Salvador Dalí masks was inspired by the surrealist artist's iconic face, symbolizing the absurdity of the system and the anonymity of the robbers. The red jumpsuits were chosen for their visual impact and uniformity, stripping away individuality to emphasize the collective identity of the team.

Music & Soundtrack

The iconic opening theme "My Life Is Going On" by Cecilia Krull became synonymous with the series. The soundtrack features a blend of Italian classics like "Bella Ciao" — a resistance anthem from WWII — which the characters sing as a symbol of rebellion and unity.

Global Reach

Netflix acquired global streaming rights in 2017 and redubbed/retitled the series as "Money Heist" for international audiences. It was promoted as a "Netflix Original" and went on to become the platform's most-watched non-English series, with over 65 million viewers worldwide.
